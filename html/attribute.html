A paragraph is a group of sentences that work together to develop a single main idea. It typically features a clear topic sentence that introduces the core subject, supporting sentences that add details, facts, or examples, and a concluding sentence that wraps up the thought. Whether it is long or short, its primary goal is to maintain unity and smooth flow so the reader can easily follow the writer's point.
